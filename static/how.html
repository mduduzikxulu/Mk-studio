How it works

Two tools. No login. Pick one from the top bar.

Mix

Add audio

Press Add audio or drop files anywhere. Add as many as you like: voice, music, effects.

Arrange

Drag clips left and right to time them, or up and down between lanes. Drag a clip's edge to trim it. Everything moves in small steps so cuts are exact.

Trim here

Click the ruler to place the playhead, then press Trim here to split. Press it again on the seam to join. Duplicate copies the selected clip (double-click works too).

Mix the levels

Set each clip's volume and the master volume. 100% is normal; up to 200% is louder. Optional: mark a clip as voice and turn on Follow the voice so music dips while it speaks.

Play, then export

Press Space to play or stop at any time. Export as WAV (best quality) or MP3 (smaller).

Made a mistake?

Undo and Redo go back and forward 50 steps. Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, S to trim, D to duplicate, Delete to remove.

Download

Choose a site and paste the link

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook. The video is found automatically.

Pick Video or Audio only

Video saves an MP4. Audio saves M4A.

Download

If your connection drops it retries and continues. Press Download again to resume.
